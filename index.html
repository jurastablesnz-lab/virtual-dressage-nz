How it works

Plan. Train. Track.

01

Plan

Add your shows and goals. Virtual Dressage NZ builds your season in phases (Foundation, Consolidate, Strength, Championship) and a weekly plan.

02

Train

Open Today for your session. Learn each test movement by movement on the arena diagram, with what the judge wants and schooling tips.

03

Track

Log results, photograph judge sheets, upload test videos for your coach and watch your scores and league points add up.

Harmony is trained, one stride at a time.

Every Grand Prix test began with a single schooling session.

Our story

From lockdown arenas to a rider’s app.

Virtual Dressage NZ didn’t start as an app. It started as a way to keep New Zealand riders riding, together, when shows stopped.

Chapter I

Born in lockdown

When Covid lockdowns stopped competitions across New Zealand, riders had nowhere to ride a test. Virtual Dressage NZ ran virtual dressage competitions: riders filmed their tests at home and they were judged online.

Chapter II

Riders return

When lockdowns lifted and riders went back to shows, the idea grew. Virtual Dressage NZ became a way to capture, share and track progress between competitions.

Chapter III

The app

That evolved into a training, planning and tracking app, now in beta and tested on Samson.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Everything is free during beta. Paid launch plans will be announced before any charges apply.

What levels is it for?

It’s built around NZ competition riders, from Novice upwards, and is currently used at Level 2.

Do I need a coach?

No. It works on your own, and if you have a coach you can plan visits and share videos with them.

What devices does it work on?

It runs in your phone or computer browser and is designed mobile-first for use at the yard and shows.

How do I join the beta?
